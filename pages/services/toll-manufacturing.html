What is Toll Manufacturing?

In toll manufacturing, you provide the raw materials, and Adept Pharma International provides:

• Advanced Manufacturing Facilities

• Skilled production teams

• Validated processes

• Quality control & analytical testing

Our 5-step process

1

Formulation Handover

GMP Production

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum batch size for toll manufacturing?
Minimum batch sizes vary by product and dosage form. For tablets/capsules, we can accommodate batches from 10,000 units. For liquids and powders, minimum quantities are based on equipment capacity. Contact us for specific requirements.
How long does the toll manufacturing process take?
Timeline depends on the product complexity and batch size. Typically, standard tablet/capsule batches take 30-45 days from material receipt to delivery. Complex formulations or high volumes may require longer timeframes. We'll provide an exact timeline during consultation.
Do you provide packaging for toll manufacturing?
Yes, we can provide secondary packaging (boxes, labels, inserts) or you can supply your own. We also offer packaging design and printing services. All packaging is done in compliance with nutraceutical labeling regulations.
What documentation is provided after manufacturing?
We provide complete batch documentation including manufacturing records, testing reports, stability data, and compliance certificates. All documentation is DRAP and export-compliant, ready for regulatory submissions.
Is NDA protection available?
Absolutely. We sign comprehensive NDAs with all toll manufacturing partners to protect your formulation and intellectual property. Your formulation details remain completely confidential.
