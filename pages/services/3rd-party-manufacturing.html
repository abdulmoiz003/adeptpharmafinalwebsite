Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the 3rd Party Manufacturing process take?
Timeline typically ranges from 90-180 days depending on formulation complexity, stability testing requirements, and packaging design. We'll provide a detailed timeline after initial consultation.
Can I modify the formulation after approval?
Yes, but modifications may require additional testing and stability data. We advise finalizing formulation before large-scale manufacturing to ensure quality consistency.
What are the minimum order quantities?
MOQs vary by product type. For tablets/capsules, minimum is typically 50,000 units. For liquids, 5,000-10,000 liters. We can discuss flexible arrangements for new brands.
Are packaging design services included?
Yes, we provide complete packaging design, label creation, and printing. All designs are compliant with pharmaceutical regulations and your brand guidelines.
What about repeat orders?
Repeat orders are significantly faster - typically 4-6 weeks for standard batches. We maintain your approved formulation and packaging specifications for consistency.
