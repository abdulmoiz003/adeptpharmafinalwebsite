Packaging & Designing Support

Creative packaging and design support services for nutraceutical, herbal, and wellness products. Our team supports partners in developing retail-ready packaging, compliant artwork, and premium branding elements that enhance product value and customer perception.

Service Overview

Why Packaging Matters in Nutraceuticals

In the healthcare and nutraceutical sector, packaging is not only about aesthetics — it is about:

• Product protection and stability
• Regulatory compliance
• Clear consumer information
• Brand credibility

Our Packaging Support Services

We provide complete assistance from concept to final packed product.

Services Include:

  • Primary & secondary packaging coordination
  • Label & box artwork designing
  • Regulatory text and information alignment
  • Packaging material selection guidance
  • Export-grade packaging support
  • Blister, bottle, sachet, jar, and tube packing coordination
  • Tamper-evident sealing systems

Types of Packaging We Support

We assist in multiple packaging formats depending on product type:

  • HDPE & PET bottles
  • Glass bottles and droppers
  • Blister & alu-alu packing
  • Sachets & pouches
  • Jars and tubs
  • Syrups and liquid bottles
  • Cartons and gift packs

Label & Artwork Designing Support

Our design team assists brands in developing:

  • Product labels
  • Box and carton artwork
  • Bottle sleeve designs
  • Export-compliant labelling
  • Multilingual packaging text (where required)

We ensure clarity, ingredient readability, and brand consistency.

Who Benefits from Packaging & Designing Support?

This service is ideal for:

  • New nutraceutical brands launching products
  • Exporters targeting international markets
  • Private label & OEM partners
  • E-commerce supplement brands

Our Process – From Idea to Finished Pack

  • Product and market discussion
  • Regulatory text and artwork planning
  • Packaging design & development
  • Material and size finalization
  • Printing & packing coordination
  • Final review and approval
