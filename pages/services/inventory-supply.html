Inventory Supply

Consistent, compliant, and ready-to-ship nutraceutical inventory

Service Overview

What Does Inventory Supply Include?

Depending on your requirement, Adept Pharma can supply:

  • Bulk manufactured tablets & capsules
  • Finished packed products ready for retail
  • White label / private label products
  • Export-compliant inventory
  • Trial batch stock for product launches

Available Dosage Forms

We supply inventory in various nutraceutical dosage formats:

Tablets
Capsules
Softgels
Sachets & nutritional powders
Effervescent granules
Liquids, syrups & suspensions
Drops & sprays
Gummies

Bulk & Semi-Finished Inventory Options

Adept Pharma can provide:

  • Bulk tablets/capsules in HDPE drums
  • Semi-finished bulk for further packing
  • Blister-packed but unlabeled product
  • Finished labeled product ready for retail

Documentation Provided (where applicable)

  • COA – Certificate of Analysis
  • MOA
  • Batch details
  • Manufacturing & expiry date
  • Product specification sheets
