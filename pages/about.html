About Adept Pharma

Our Story

Who We Are

Founded in 2010, Adept Pharma has grown into a trusted pharmaceutical and nutraceutical manufacturing company, serving brands across Pakistan and international markets.

Based in Rawat Industrial Area, our state-of-the-art facility combines advanced technology, strict quality standards, and industry expertise to deliver reliable manufacturing solutions.

Our journey has been defined by unwavering commitment to quality, integrity, and the health of end-users. Every product manufactured at Adept Pharma carries the promise of excellence and compliance.

Meet Our CEO

Shoaib Mehmood Malik - CEO Adept Pharma

Shoaib Mehmood Malik, CEO of Adept Pharma, brings decades of pharmaceutical experience and a relentless focus on quality, compliance, and customer satisfaction. Under his leadership, Adept Pharma has grown into a trusted partner for both local and international brands.

He focuses on innovation, operational excellence, and a people-first culture, ensuring every product meets the highest standards while serving the evolving needs of patients and partners.
